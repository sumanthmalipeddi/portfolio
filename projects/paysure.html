PaySure — payment reconciliation for gig workers

Next.js 16 · TypeScript · SQLite/Drizzle · OAuth 2.0 · Source on GitHub ↗

Problem

Gig workers in India get paid against hours their employers compute — and have no practical way to verify the math, the pay-period boundaries, or the TDS deducted. Underpayment hides in timezone edges and rounding. I wrote the product spec myself and built to it.

Build

The app converts Google Calendar events into employer-format payout statements and reconciles them against actual payments — flagging underpaid hours and TDS deductions. 12 routes, 59 unit tests, OAuth 2.0, PDF/CSV export.

The correctness core is fintech-grade: a timezone-correct pay-period engine for India's 26th–25th cycles with explicit UTC/IST boundary tests, integer-paise money arithmetic (no floats near money, ever), three-bucket reconciliation, and TDS-aware (§194J) verification against a Form 26AS ledger.

Security was a design input, not a patch: OAuth with read-only scope, AES-256-GCM-encrypted refresh tokens, CSRF state validation, and calendar descriptions deliberately never stored. A from-scratch ICS parser with RRULE expansion serves as the no-OAuth fallback.

Outcomes

  • 59 tests including UTC/IST pay-period boundary cases
  • Integer paise end-to-end — zero floating-point money paths
  • §194J TDS verification reconciled against Form 26AS
  • Read-only OAuth with AES-256-GCM token encryption; descriptions never stored
